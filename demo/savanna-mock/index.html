Lesson 1: Why accessible tech education matters

Lesson video

Sign window: British Sign Language avatar (experimental). Made by a machine from the English captions, not by an interpreter, and not checked by BSL signers. It signs the words that have a BSL sign (about a third of them) and keeps pace with the lesson. To see every word signed (the others are fingerspelled), choose Wait for the signer in the Sign language settings: the video then pauses after each caption until the signer finishes. BSL is not Ethiopian Sign Language. Non-commercial demonstration.

The British Sign Language avatar videos have not been made on this server yet (sh scripts/bsl-video/make.sh).

About this lesson

In this short talk from the Digital Bridge training session, the speaker explains why ALX wants its learning platforms to work for everyone, including people with disabilities, refugees and internally displaced people.
