Package D: caption reports, Amharic keyboard, sign answers, live sessions

Report a caption problem

Who this helps: every learner, especially Deaf learners who notice a wrong Amharic caption. Staff see the reports in Fidel Studio.

Loading the lesson video…

Try it:

  • Play the video, then press "Report a caption problem". The video pauses; the time and the caption shown are filled in.
  • Choose what is wrong, write a correction if you like, and press Send. Escape closes the dialog and the video plays on.
  • Turn your connection off and send a report: it is saved on the device and sent when you are back online.

Amharic keyboard

Who this helps: refugees and learners on shared or borrowed computers with no Amharic keyboard installed, and anyone who cannot remember the key combinations.

Letters typed: 0

Try it:

  • Press "Show Amharic keyboard". Tab to the letters; arrow keys move between them, Home and End jump to the first and last.
  • Choose ለ, then ሉ: "ሉ" is added at the cursor and you are back at the letters. Escape goes back from the forms to the letters.
  • "Delete one letter" removes one whole letter; "Back to the text" puts you in the text box.
