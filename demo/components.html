Package D: caption reports, Amharic keyboard, sign answers, live sessions

Report a caption problem

Who this helps: every learner, especially Deaf learners who notice a wrong Amharic caption. Staff see the reports in Fidel Studio.

Loading the lesson video…

Try it:

  • Play the video, then press "Report a caption problem". The video pauses; the time and the caption shown are filled in.
  • Choose what is wrong, write a correction if you like, and press Send. Escape closes the dialog and the video plays on.
  • Turn your connection off and send a report: it is saved on the device and sent when you are back online.

Amharic keyboard

Who this helps: refugees and learners on shared or borrowed computers with no Amharic keyboard installed, and anyone who cannot remember the key combinations.

Letters typed: 0

Try it:

  • Press "Show Amharic keyboard". Tab to the letters; arrow keys move between them, Home and End jump to the first and last.
  • Choose ለ, then ሉ: "ሉ" is added at the cursor and you are back at the letters. Escape goes back from the forms to the letters.
  • "Delete one letter" removes one whole letter; "Back to the text" puts you in the text box.

Live session card

Who this helps: Deaf, hard-of-hearing and blind learners deciding whether they can follow a live class, and asking for the support they need.

Try it:

  • Read the access line: captions, sign language and spoken languages are written in words.
  • Press "Request access support", tick what you need and Send. Only the kind of support is asked, never why.
  • "Add to calendar" downloads a calendar file. The Join link appears 15 minutes before the start; after the end, "Watch the recording".

Answer in sign language

Who this helps: Deaf learners whose first language is Ethiopian Sign Language, to reply, ask a question or explain a caption problem without writing.

Try it:

  • Press "Start camera": only now does the browser ask for the camera. "Camera on" shows while it is on; "Stop camera" turns it off.
  • Press Record: a 3-2-1 countdown, then "Recording". Stop recording turns the camera off. Watch it, slower if you like, then Record again or Send.
  • With no camera (or the camera refused), the message says so and offers your phone's camera app or a video file instead.

Reading guide

Who this helps: low-vision learners at 200 to 400% zoom, and learners with dyslexia or attention difficulties.

A web page is a text file that a browser turns into what you see. The text is written in HTML.

HTML uses tags, such as a heading tag and a paragraph tag, to say what each part of the page is.

  • Headings help screen-reader users jump through a page.
  • Paragraphs hold ordinary text.
  • Links take you to another place.

ድረ-ገጽ አሳሹ ወደሚያዩት ገጽ የሚቀይረው የጽሑፍ ፋይል ነው።

Try it:

  • Press "Reading guide". Move the pointer, click in the text or Tab to the link: the block you are in is outlined.
  • Use "Next line" and "Previous line" to move block by block. Choose "Dim the rest" to fade the other blocks.
  • Your choice is remembered on this device.
