Deaf learners, motor access, navigation and help

A tech word with its sign: <fidel-sign-term>

Who this helps: Deaf learners, for whom written English or Amharic is often a second language.

To open your account, you log in. Your lessons are kept on a server.

Placeholder: the video for “log in” is a colour-bar test pattern, not a real sign. The team will record Ethiopian Sign Language clips with interpreters. “server” shows what a learner sees when no sign has been recorded yet.

  • Press Tab to the word “log in” and press Enter. The sign plays, muted, with Pause and Slower buttons and the meaning in English and Amharic.
  • Press Escape: the dialog closes and you are back on the word.

Sounds you can see: <fidel-visual-alert>

Who this helps: Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners, who miss sounds that tell other people something happened.

  • Press “Show a test alert”: a yellow banner appears and stays until you press its Close alert button.
  • Open “Alert settings” to let alerts close after 15 seconds, or to vibrate (on phones that can).

Big Previous and Next links: <fidel-step-nav>

Who this helps: learners with tremor, one hand, or a switch, who need large targets that say where they go.

  • Press Tab to the Previous and Next links; each says the lesson it opens. They are at least 48 pixels tall and full width on a phone.

Never lose work to a time-out: <fidel-session-keeper>

Who this helps: everyone who types slowly or uses assistive technology, so a form never times out on them.

No session events yet.

  • Press “Start a 40-second test session”. After 10 seconds a dialog asks “Do you need more time?” with “Yes, more time” focused.
  • Press Enter for more time, or open “Time limit” and tick “Never time me out”.

Where am I, and jump anywhere: <fidel-lesson-map>

Who this helps: blind learners who move by headings and landmarks, and everyone in a long lesson.

Help in the same place, in every form: <fidel-help>

Who this helps: everyone; text comes first so Deaf learners are never sent to a phone line.

The same help in Amharic:

  • Press “Get help”: the dialog lists text chat first and the phone (voice call) last, each a real link.
  • Press Escape to close it; focus returns to “Get help”.
